How to read the averages

Press points: Each session is converted to /100, then the recorded sessions are averaged with equal weight. September 30 excluded the teamwork category, so its maximum was 100; the earlier sessions were out of 110.

Quiz points: The average uses the awarded /15 scores from the three closed quizzes. Missing quizzes are excluded. Recorded partial attempts count; Umair’s Issue 333 score is from a partial attempt. Counts therefore indicate quiz records, not completed quizzes.

Averages are rounded to one decimal place after calculation. Previously awarded quiz rounding and facilitator corrections are preserved, including Sarah’s 14/15 for Issue 334. The open Session 4 quiz, due October 7, is not yet included.
