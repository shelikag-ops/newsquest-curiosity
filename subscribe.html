Three steps to get your family reading The Week Junior, US edition.

Shelika’s recommendation: choose print + digital.
Printed copies take time to reach your mailbox. Digital access makes it easier to read the assigned issue—and revisit earlier issues—while you wait.
Step 1

Choose your subscription

Go to theweekjunior.com and select Subscribe now. Choose the US edition and the plan that suits your family.

Use an email address you can easily access and keep your order confirmation. The publisher advises allowing 2–4 weeks for the first printed issue.

Visit The Week Junior
Step 2

Find your subscription account number

After ordering, look for the publisher’s confirmation or welcome email. Check your spam or promotions folder if you do not see it.

Visit service.theweekjunior.com. Choose Login Using Your Email Address and ZIP Code, using the email address and ZIP code associated with your subscription. Follow any instructions in your welcome email, then note your account/customer number from your subscription details.

If your new order is not recognised, contact the publisher rather than buying another subscription.

Open customer service
Step 3

Open the digital magazine

Go to magazine.theweekjunior.com. If prompted to sign in, use your subscription account/customer number and follow the on-screen instructions.

Open the Magazine tab and choose the issue assigned for NewsQuest. Earlier issues are available in the archive, so the assigned issue may not be the newest cover on the page.

Open the digital reader

Ready for NewsQuest

Children read a few pages beforehand—usually pages 2–8, which cover the bigger stories happening around the world. Check the weekly update for the assigned issue and any particular stories to focus on.

As they read, encourage them to notice one interesting idea, one thing they do not understand, and one question they would like to bring to the group.
