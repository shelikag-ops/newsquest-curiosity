A note for parents

We ran out of time for our main stories on Wednesday, so here they are as games. Every mission is optional, and kids can do them in any order, a few at a time. Most take 10 to 15 minutes. Answers save automatically for your child's club leader.

The 9/11 case files focus on the helpers, but the topic can feel heavy. You may want to preview Mission 2 and do it together. The block tower in Mission 5 needs a grown-up nearby. If you upload a photo, please show only the drawing or tower, not your child's face.
