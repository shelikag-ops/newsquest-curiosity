How NewsQuest press points work

Press points recognize preparation, thoughtful contributions, curiosity and learning. Quality matters more than how often a child speaks. Written and quieter contributions count too.

  • On the Dot · 10
    On time, camera on and ready.
  • On Air · 20
    Contributing to discussion.
  • Ready to Report · 20
    Reading and preparing.
  • Curiosity Spark · 20
    Thoughtful questions.
  • Detail Detectives · 20
    Noticing facts others missed.
  • Better Together · 10
    Teamwork and presenting, when included in the session’s scoring.
  • Beyond the Headlines · 10
    Exploring beyond the assigned reading.

The usual maximum is 110. The newsletter states the applicable maximum for each session.

Quiz scores and totals

The newsletter shows reviewed quiz scores on a /15 scale. A dash means no attempt was found under a matched name; it is not a zero-performance result. Where a combined total is shown, it includes only recorded points. Scores are for the identified session and quiz, not cumulative across weeks.

Parents are welcome to ask for their own child’s points breakdown. Individual student scores are not published on this guide page.
